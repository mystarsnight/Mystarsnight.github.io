Find the right place to search.

Mystarsnight Quick Search helps you decide where to continue your search. Different websites are useful for different things, and Quick Search compares your request with categories to recommend a starting point.

How Mystarsnight Quick Search Works

Quick Search is designed as a simple starting tool. Instead of assuming that one website is always best, it looks at the words in your request and compares them with different types of destinations.

1

Describe what you want

Enter a short search such as learning a skill, finding a product, researching a topic, watching a video, or finding programming resources.

2

Compare destinations

Quick Search checks the type of words in your request and ranks destinations that may be useful for that type of search.

3

Choose where to go

You can visit any suggested destination yourself. The recommended destination can also open automatically unless you cancel the redirect.

What Makes Quick Search Different?

Mystarsnight Quick Search is built around a simple idea: the best place to continue searching depends on what you are trying to do.

Purpose-Based

The recommendations are based on the purpose of your search rather than showing the same destination for every request.

Simple to Use

There are no accounts required for basic searching. Enter what you want and Quick Search gives you a clear set of possible next steps.

You Stay in Control

You can manually choose a destination or cancel an automatic redirect. The optional game can also be skipped.

About Mystarsnight

Mystarsnight is an independent web project focused on experimenting with useful browser tools, games, and interactive experiences.

Mystarsnight Quick Search was created to provide a simple tool for deciding where a search may be most useful. A programming question, a product search, a video search, and a general research question may each benefit from a different starting destination.

The goal is to keep the experience straightforward, understandable, and useful for visitors.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Mystarsnight Quick Search handles information while you use the website.

Search Requests

The words you enter into Quick Search are used by the webpage to generate recommendations. When you choose or are redirected to another website, that website may receive the search terms as part of the destination URL.

Advertising

This website may use third-party advertising services. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

Third-Party Websites

Quick Search may direct visitors to third-party websites. Mystarsnight does not control the content, privacy practices, or policies of external websites. Visitors should review the policies of websites they visit.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website's features or practices change.
